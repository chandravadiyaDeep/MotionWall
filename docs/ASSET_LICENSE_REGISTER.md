## Rules

- Original work: record creator and ownership confirmation.
- Commissioned work: retain contract/permission covering app distribution.
- Public-domain work: record the authoritative source and public-domain basis.
- Third-party licensed work: verify redistribution, modification, and commercial-use terms.
- Anime characters, manga panels, film scenes, music, and fan edits are not automatically free to redistribute.
- Never assume that content being publicly viewable means it can be bundled in an APK.
